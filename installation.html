How It Works

Our Installation Process

A proven 8-step process that takes you from inquiry to a fully protected property.

1

Free Site Survey

Our security expert visits your property to carry out a free, no-obligation site survey. We assess the layout, entry points and your specific security needs.

2

Security Assessment

We conduct a thorough assessment of vulnerable areas, lighting, access points and identify the level of protection your property requires.

3

Product Recommendation

Based on your assessment, we recommend the ideal products and package — matching your budget, layout and security requirements without over-selling.

4

Installation

Our certified technicians install all equipment with precision — camera positioning, secure wiring, and clean cable management for a professional finish.

5

Configuration

We configure your system — connecting cameras, setting up the mobile app, syncing smart features, and tailoring settings to your preferences.

6

Testing

We run comprehensive tests on every camera, sensor, alarm and connection. Your system is verified to work flawlessly before handover.

7

Customer Handover

We walk you through every feature, provide documentation and ensure you're confident managing your new security system.

8

After-Sales Support

Our team is available 24/7 for maintenance, updates, troubleshooting and any questions you may have long after installation.
